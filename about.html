About Us

The story behind Janki PG

Every parent knows that silence when a child first leaves home. My daughter is away at college, and I still wonder — is she safe, has she eaten, would someone notice if something felt wrong?

That question became Janki PG — a home built on one test: would this be good enough for my own child?

If you're a parent weighing this decision, I understand exactly what you're feeling. That trust means everything to us.
